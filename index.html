Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions parents ask about our institution and policies.

We offer the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) for our Early Foundation and Primary school grades. For older candidates, we offer standard secondary study tracks transitioning into Junior and Senior Secondary stages.

Our school is situated in Jomvu-Jitoni, Mombasa, Kenya. We offer a quiet and secure learning environment away from urban congestion.

Co-curricular sessions are built directly into our weekly timetable. Learners participate in sports clubs, science labs, and performance arts training with dedicated coaches.

Edward Kings Academy is a co-educational (mixed) day school. We do not offer boarding facilities, and all students attend as day scholars.
